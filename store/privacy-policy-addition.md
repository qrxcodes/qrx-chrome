# Privacy policy: browser extension section

The store forms declare three kinds of data: the tab's address (web history), the prompt and style, and the sign-in
token. Store reviewers compare those answers with the published policy, so https://qrx.codes/privacy needs a section
that describes them the same way. Suggested text, to sit next to the existing section on AI assistants:

---

### QRX browser extension (Chrome and Edge)

When you use the QRX extension for Chrome or Edge:

- **The page's address.** When you click the QRX button, the extension reads the address of the tab you are on and
  shows it as the link for your code. It is sent to us only if you create a code with it, and we store it as that
  code's destination, as we do for codes made on the website. The extension does not read the content of the page,
  other tabs or your browsing history.
- **Your prompt and style.** The picture description and style you choose are sent to us to make the code and are
  stored with the code. The extension keeps them in your browser's extension storage until the code is finished.
- **Your sign-in.** When you sign in, the extension receives a sign-in token from our sign-in provider (or uses an API
  key you paste into its settings). It keeps the token or key in your browser's extension storage and sends it only to
  us, to identify your account. Signing out of the extension deletes it from the browser.

The extension has no advertising, analytics or tracking, and we do not sell or share this information. It is used
only to make and show your QR codes.
